Data Retention and Deletion Policy

How long we keep information and how it is securely disposed of.

1. Retention schedule2. Review3. Secure disposal4. Ownership
This document is published in English. If you need help understanding it, please contact us and we will explain it in your language.
Version 1.0Last updated 4 November 2026Owner MUKTalent Healthcare management

1. Retention schedule

RecordRetention
Candidates not placed12 months after the last meaningful contact, unless the candidate asks us to keep their profile
Candidates placed with an employerAt least 1 year after our last supply of services (Conduct Regulations 2003); up to 6 years after the placement ends for legal claims
Candidate documents (identity, qualifications, references)Deleted when no longer needed for the purpose requested, and at the latest with the candidate record
Employer and general enquiries24 months after the last contact
Complaints register6 years after closure
Audit and access logs6 years
Marketing consent recordsUntil consent is withdrawn, plus 2 years as evidence
Employee recordsSee the Employee Privacy Notice

2. Review

Our recruitment system flags records that have reached the end of their retention period. An administrator reviews the list each month and deletes or anonymises records that are no longer required.

3. Secure disposal

  • Electronic files are permanently deleted from storage.
  • Database records are deleted or irreversibly anonymised, keeping only the reference number and non-identifying statistics.
  • Physical documents are shredded by cross-cut shredder or a certified confidential waste service.
  • Each deletion is recorded in the audit log.

4. Ownership

The person responsible for data protection reviews this schedule at least once a year.
