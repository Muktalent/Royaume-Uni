Website Privacy Policy

How we use information when you visit this website or contact us.

1. Who we are

The controller of your personal information is MAGHREB UK TALENT LTD (company number 16987132), trading as MUKTalent Healthcare, registered office Flat 3b Flitton House, The Sutton Estate, London, England, N1 1UG. Contact: agents@muktalent.com, +44 73 1187 4281.

2. Information we collect through this website

  • Enquiry forms (candidate, employer, general and complaints): your name, contact details, organisation, job title and the content of your message.
  • Candidate registration: the information described in our Candidate Privacy Notice.
  • Technical information: limited security logs created by our hosting provider (such as IP address and browser type) to protect the website against abuse.
  • Analytics: only if you accept analytics cookies, anonymous statistics about the pages you visit.

3. Why we use it and our lawful bases

PurposeLawful basis (UK GDPR)
Responding to enquiries and providing our recruitment servicesLegitimate interests; steps taken at your request before entering into a contract
Handling complaintsLegitimate interests; legal obligation
Website security and spam protectionLegitimate interests
Marketing updatesConsent, which you can withdraw at any time
AnalyticsConsent

4. Who we share it with

Your information is accessible only to authorised MUKTalent Healthcare personnel who need it for their role. We use the following service providers, who act on our instructions under written contracts:

ProviderPurpose
SupabaseSecure database, authentication and encrypted document storage for our recruitment system
VercelWebsite hosting and secure (HTTPS) delivery
ResendSending confirmation and notification emails
Google Analytics (only with your consent)Anonymous website usage statistics

We do not sell personal information.

5. International transfers

Some of our service providers may process information outside the UK. Where this happens we assess the applicable legal requirements and rely on UK adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, together with additional safeguards where needed. When you ask us to, and only with your agreement, we may also share your profile with an employer in the UK.

6. How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for up to 24 months after our last contact. Complaints are kept for 6 years. Full details are in our Data Retention and Deletion Policy.

7. Security

The website uses HTTPS throughout. Our recruitment system uses individual user accounts, role-based permissions, multi-factor authentication for team access, private encrypted storage for documents and activity logs. See How we protect personal information.

8. Your rights

  • the right to be informed about how we use your information;
  • the right of access to a copy of your information;
  • the right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • the right to erasure, where applicable;
  • the right to restrict processing, where applicable;
  • the right to data portability, where applicable;
  • the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing;
  • rights in relation to automated decision-making (we do not make recruitment decisions solely by automated means);
  • the right to withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

To exercise any right, email agents@muktalent.com or, if you are a candidate, use Help & Support → My data in the candidate portal. We respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

9. Changes

We review this policy at least once a year and publish any changes on this page.
