For CandidatesInternational healthcare professionals

Your next role in UK healthcare, with honest guidance.

We support qualified nurses, allied health professionals and other healthcare staff who want to work in the UK. You will get clear information, a named consultant and a private portal to follow every step.

Register as a CandidateView vacancies
Please read before applying. We cannot guarantee employment, visa approval, professional registration or a specific salary. Employers make hiring decisions, regulators decide registration and the Home Office decides visa applications. We will always be honest about your options.

01What you need to know

Everything we will discuss with you.

These are the topics your consultant will cover. Each one depends on your profession, your country of training and the role.

Available healthcare professions

Nursing, allied health, pharmacy and social care. See the requirements for each profession before you apply.

Qualification requirements

UK employers and regulators need evidence of your qualifications, training and recent practice. We explain what is usually requested.

Professional registration

Regulated professions need UK registration, such as with the NMC or HCPC. Your qualification alone does not allow you to practise in the UK.

English-language requirements

Where applicable, regulators and the Home Office set English language requirements. We tell you which tests are accepted for your route.

Recruitment and interview stages

You will know your stage at all times, and we help you prepare for each interview.

Employment conditions

We share accurate information from the employer on pay, hours, contract type and conditions before you accept anything.

Visa and immigration considerations

Most international professionals need a visa sponsored by the employer. The Home Office decides every application.

Relocation and pre-departure guidance

Where offered, we share practical guidance on accommodation, travel and your first weeks in the UK.

02Your candidate portal

Follow your progress, privately and securely.

After you register, you can create an account with the same email address to access your private portal.

  • See your current stage in the recruitment journey
  • Update your details and preferences
  • Upload documents securely when your consultant requests them
  • Read and send messages to your consultant
  • View interview dates and preparation notes
  • Access recruitment guidance and support

03Our promise to you

Fair recruitment is not optional.

No candidate fees

Candidates are not charged recruitment or work-finding fees by MUKTalent Healthcare. If anyone asks you for a fee in our name, tell us immediately.

Only what is needed

At registration we ask only for your CV and basic details. We request identity and qualification documents later, only when they are needed for a specific step.

A voice if things go wrong

You can raise a concern or complaint at any time, and we explain the independent organisations you can contact.
