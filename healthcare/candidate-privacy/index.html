Candidate Privacy Notice

How we use the information of healthcare candidates who register or apply with us.

1. Who we are2. Information we collect3. How we use it4. Who we share it with5. International transfers6. How long we keep it7. Security8. Your rights9. Fees
This document is published in English. If you need help understanding it, please contact us and we will explain it in your language.
Version 1.0Last updated 4 November 2026Owner MUKTalent Healthcare management

1. Who we are

The controller of your personal information is MAGHREB UK TALENT LTD (company number 16987132), trading as MUKTalent Healthcare, registered office Flat 3b Flitton House, The Sutton Estate, London, England, N1 1UG. Contact: agents@muktalent.com, +44 73 1187 4281. MUKTalent Healthcare acts as an employment agency when it introduces candidates to employers.

2. Information we collect

At registration we ask only for what we need to assess your profile: your name, email address, telephone number, country of residence, nationality (optional), healthcare profession, years of experience, professional registration status, English language level, current employment status, preferred UK location, your CV and any additional information you choose to give.

Later in the process, only when needed, your consultant may ask you to upload specific documents through the secure portal, for example qualification certificates, registration evidence, English test results, references, or identity and right-to-work documents requested by an employer. We do not ask for sensitive documents at registration.

Special category and criminal offence information. We do not ask for health information or criminal records. These checks, such as occupational health and DBS checks, are carried out by the employer. If you choose to tell us something sensitive, we will only use it where necessary, for example to make reasonable adjustments for an interview.

4. Who we share it with

  • Prospective employers: only after you have agreed to be considered for a specific role or employer.
  • Professional verification providers and referees: to confirm qualifications, registration and employment history.
  • Authorised recruitment partners: only where necessary, under written agreements and appropriate safeguards.

  • Regulators and authorities: where the law requires it.

5. International transfers

Some of our service providers may process information outside the UK. Where this happens we assess the applicable legal requirements and rely on UK adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, together with additional safeguards where needed. When you ask us to, and only with your agreement, we may also share your profile with an employer in the UK.

6. How long we keep it

If you are not placed, we keep your record for 12 months after our last meaningful contact, then securely delete or anonymise it unless you ask us to keep it. If you are placed, we keep the records the law requires for at least one year after our last supply of services, and up to 6 years after the placement ends to deal with legal claims. Details: Data Retention and Deletion Policy.

7. Security

Your documents are stored in private, encrypted storage and can only be opened through short-lived secure links by you and by the authorised team members managing your application. Each consultant can see only the candidates assigned to them. Every access to documents and every change to your record is logged.

8. Your rights

  • the right to be informed about how we use your information;
  • the right of access to a copy of your information;
  • the right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • the right to erasure, where applicable;
  • the right to restrict processing, where applicable;
  • the right to data portability, where applicable;
  • the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing;
  • rights in relation to automated decision-making (we do not make recruitment decisions solely by automated means);
  • the right to withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

To exercise any right, email agents@muktalent.com or, if you are a candidate, use Help & Support → My data in the candidate portal. We respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

9. Fees

Candidates are not charged recruitment or work-finding fees by MUKTalent Healthcare.
