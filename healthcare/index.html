01Who We Are

The healthcare recruitment division of MUKTalent, operating from the United Kingdom.

MUKTalent has supported international talent coming to the UK since its foundation. MUKTalent Healthcare applies the same care to healthcare and social care recruitment, with its own team, processes and standards.

We work with qualified professionals who want to build a career in the UK, and with UK employers who need reliable, ethically recruited staff. Our role is to make the process clear, fair and well organised for everyone involved.

04Our Recruitment Approach

A clear path in eight stages.

Every candidate follows the same documented process. You can see your current stage at any time in the candidate portal.

  1. 1

    Initial Registration

    You register online and receive a candidate reference number.

  2. 2

    Candidate Assessment

    A consultant reviews your experience, goals and eligibility.

  3. 3

    Documentation and Qualification Verification

    We check qualifications, registration status and references.

  4. 4

    Employer Matching

    Where appropriate, we present your profile to suitable employers.

  5. 5

    Interviews and Selection

    We coordinate interviews and help you prepare.

  6. 6

    Employment Offer

    The employer makes any offer directly; we help you understand it.

  7. 7

    Pre-employment and Immigration Preparation

    Employer checks, sponsorship and visa steps.

  8. 8

    Pre-departure Support

    Practical guidance before you travel, where offered.

05Ethical Recruitment

Candidates are not charged recruitment or work-finding fees by MUKTalent Healthcare.

We follow the Code of Practice for the International Recruitment of Health and Social Care Personnel in England. We check the current red and amber country lists before any international recruitment, and we do not actively recruit from red-list countries.

Our Commitment to Ethical Recruitment
Fair and transparent recruitment
Equal treatment and non-discrimination
Accurate job and employment information
No coercion, exploitation or misleading promises
Qualification and reference verification
An accessible complaints procedure
