Ethical RecruitmentCode of Practice

Our Commitment to Ethical International Recruitment

We follow the Code of Practice for the International Recruitment of Health and Social Care Personnel in England. Here we explain what that means in practice and how you can hold us to it.

Candidate feesOur clearest promise

Candidates are not charged recruitment or work-finding fees by MUKTalent Healthcare.

Charging work-seekers for finding them work is prohibited by the Employment Agencies Act 1973 and is against the Code of Practice. If anyone asks you for money in our name, please report it to us straight away.

No registration, placement or work-finding fees
No fees deducted from your salary by us
No unfair repayment or exit clauses agreed by us with employers
Report concerns to agents@muktalent.com

02International recruitment restrictions

We check country lists before we recruit.

The Code of Practice includes a list of countries, based on the World Health Organization’s health workforce support and safeguards list.

  • Red-list countries: we do not undertake active recruitment.
  • Amber-list countries: recruitment only follows the applicable government-to-government agreement and its conditions.
  • Our system checks the candidate’s country of residence at registration and blocks employer matching where recruitment is not permitted.
  • We review the official list regularly and whenever it is updated.

Current red and amber list on NHS Employers

If you live in a red-list country

We cannot actively recruit you or present you to employers. The Code allows you to apply directly to UK employers on your own initiative, for example through NHS Jobs.

If you register with us, we will tell you this honestly and will not process your profile for matching.

NHS Jobs

Ethical Recruiters List

NHS Employers publishes the Ethical Recruiters List of organisations that have committed to the Code. MUKTalent Healthcare is not currently on the list. Our policies and systems are designed to meet its requirements, and we will only state that we are listed once this has been formally confirmed.

03Complaints and accountability

Clear responsibility, independent routes.

Our Ethical Recruitment and Compliance Lead is responsible for this policy and for every complaint we receive.

Dedicated contact

Complaints can be made online, by email to agents@muktalent.com or by phone. We acknowledge every complaint within 2 working days.

Written procedure and register

Every complaint receives a reference number, is recorded in our internal complaints register and is investigated by someone not involved in the matter.

Escalation

If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Employment Agency Standards Inspectorate or, for Code of Practice concerns, NHS Employers.
