International recruitment, handled responsibly.

02Working together

What we ask of our employer partners.

Ethical recruitment depends on both sides. Before we recruit for you, we agree the following in writing.

  • Accurate job descriptions, pay and employment conditions
  • No fees or costs passed to candidates, and no unfair repayment clauses
  • Recruitment only from countries permitted by the Code of Practice
  • Fair, non-discriminatory selection and appropriate pastoral support
  • Valid sponsor licence and CQC registration, where they apply

Any recruitment fees are agreed with the employer in writing before work begins. Candidates are never charged.
