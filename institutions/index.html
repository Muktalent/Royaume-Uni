A FOCUSED REMIT

Start with the student.
Build a coherent project.

A useful introduction is more than a name on a list.

Our initial assessment gathers the student's study goals, academic background, English level, intended intake and budget. These details provide a basis for a conversation, not an automatic eligibility decision.

We distinguish information declared by the student from information checked against documents or programme requirements. Admissions remain subject to the institution's own review and decisions.

STRUCTURED FOLLOW-UP

From an enquiry
to an informed next step.

  1. 01

    Capture the study project

    A structured profile records the student's objectives and circumstances. General enquiries are kept distinct from candidate records.

  2. 02

    Discuss readiness and open questions

    An initial conversation helps identify missing information, documentation needs and the options to examine.

  3. 03

    Prepare the agreed next steps

    Internal follow-up records the current phase, responsibility and next action. A review-ready profile is not presented as an application submitted or an offer received.

  4. 04

    Agree an institution-specific process

    Before any representation or application handling under a proposed collaboration, the parties should agree the scope, submission route, responsibilities and reporting requirements.

CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

What a collaboration
should make explicit.

01 /

Scope & representation

Markets, programmes, authorised activities and brand permissions should be agreed in writing. This website does not imply a direct contract with every institution a student may consider.

02 /

Fees & responsibilities

Student-facing service terms, any charges and relevant commercial relationships should be explained clearly. Support does not guarantee an admission, scholarship or visa.

03 /

Data & reporting

Any exchange of candidate information must have an appropriate purpose and lawful basis. Internal records and documents are not published on this website.

PIPELINE, WITH CONTEXT

Evidence over
headline numbers.

Enquiries, assessed profiles, submitted applications and enrolments are different things.

Our current pipeline reporting describes prospective candidate records and their internal assessment phases. We do not turn those records into public claims of applications, offers or enrolments.

Any figures used in partnership discussions should be dated, reviewed for test records and duplicates, and explained with a clear scope. A record count is not automatically a count of unique people.

Candidate-level information is not made publicly downloadable. The detail and conditions of any institutional reporting should be agreed separately.
